## The Noise of Social Media

We live in a world where information is constantly being shared, consumed, and forgotten. Social media has made it easier than ever to express ideas, but I often find that meaningful thoughts deserve more than a few lines in a post.

With the constant flow of content, trends, and updates, it can sometimes be difficult to slow down and give an idea the attention it deserves. I wanted something different — a space where I could take my time, explore topics in depth, and express my thoughts without being surrounded by the constant noise of social media.

## A Space of My Own

I'm Majed, someone with a deep passion for technology and a constant curiosity to learn new things. I enjoy exploring new ideas, understanding how things work, and discovering how technology continues to shape the world around us.

Building my own blog felt like a natural step. Rather than relying entirely on social media platforms, I wanted to create a space that I could shape and develop in my own way. A place where I could write freely, share ideas, and focus on the content itself rather than trends, engagement, or visibility.

## A Reflection of My Interests

I don't want this blog to be limited to one subject. While cybersecurity is a major part of my professional interests, technology is a much broader world, and there is always something new worth exploring.

Here, I hope to write about different topics, including cybersecurity, threat intelligence, OSINT, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies. I also want to share interesting tools, discoveries, technical concepts, and perspectives on how technology affects businesses and everyday life.

Beyond technical topics, I would like to share personal experiences, including certifications I complete, tools I explore, and lessons I learn throughout my journey.

## Learning Through Sharing

For me, writing is not just about sharing what I already know. It is also a way to learn, reflect, and understand things more deeply.

Researching a topic, organizing information, and putting ideas into words can reveal new perspectives and questions that might otherwise go unnoticed. I believe that documenting experiences and sharing knowledge can be valuable not only to others but also to personal growth.

I don't believe you need to be an expert in everything to share something meaningful. Sometimes, sharing a new discovery or a lesson learned along the way can be just as valuable.

## Looking Ahead

I see this blog as an ongoing journey rather than a finished project. Its content will grow and change alongside my interests, experiences, and knowledge.

My goal is simple: to create a space where I can explore what interests me, document what I learn, and share ideas that I find worth discussing.

If something sparks my curiosity, there is a good chance it might find its way here.

**Welcome to my space beyond social media.**
